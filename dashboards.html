Cody Heart / Dashboards

Two questions, watched live.

One tracks the war on Gaza. The other maps who holds American wealth — and who doesn’t. Both began as something I kept checking on.

CompiledApril 2026
UpdatedLive · refreshed continuously
Built withClaude · Anthropic

History tells you how a subject got here. Live data shows you where it stands today. These dashboards are my attempt to turn two subjects I kept following into something I can watch change in real time. Built with help from Claude, the AI assistant from Anthropic. They’re exploratory rather than journalistic — start here, then go to the sources for the real context.
